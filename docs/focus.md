# Keeping attention on the content

The public pages have one job each: read the thing, answer, done. The
rules that follow exist so nothing on the page competes with that.

**One primary action per screen.** A page that offers two equally
weighted buttons has asked the visitor to make a decision before the
one it wanted.

**The form is the page.** No sidebar, no related items, no navigation
away from a page somebody was linked to.

**Nothing moves on its own.** No carousels, no autoplay, no animation
that starts without a click.

**The privacy line sits where it applies**, above the field that asks
for an address, not behind a link.

**Ads, where they run at all, sit under the content and never inside
it** (`docs/ads.md`), and never on an organisation's pages.
