Every event has a link and a QR code. Anyone who opens it can sign up, without an account.

## Steps

1. Open your event. The link is at the top right, with a copy button next to it.

    ![The link at the top right of the event's page](details.event.2)

2. Click the copy button and paste the link in the group chat, a mail or on the website.

3. Making a poster? Click the picture under it. That is a QR code, and it is copied as an image.

    ![The QR code under the link](details.event.3)

## You now see

Under the title is the **Sign-ups** block. Every sign-up lands there, with the name and the party size. The number on the right adds up.

![The Sign-ups block on the event's page](details.event.1)

**The link does not work for somebody?** Check that the event is not in the archive. An archived event has no working link.

## Questions

**What does somebody fill in?** A name, real or made up, and how many people they bring. If you switched on **How did you find us?**, they also pick where they know you from. The page asks nothing more.

**Can somebody withdraw?** Yes. After signing up, everyone is shown their own link, once. With that link they change their sign-up or withdraw it. You need do nothing.

**Somebody lost that link.** In the Sign-ups block, click **View sign-ups** and then the link button next to the name. You make a new link for that person, and the old one stops working. Their page will say that an organiser did this.

**Do I see email addresses?** No. If somebody leaves an address, only the app sees it, and only to send the mail you switched on.
