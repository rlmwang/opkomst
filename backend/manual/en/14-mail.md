---
title: What happens to an email address?
description: What the app does with an email address, how long it keeps it, and when it is gone.
audience: organisation
---
A participant can leave an email address for a reminder or a questionnaire afterwards. This chapter says exactly what happens to it.

## When an address is asked for

Only when you switched on one of the two mails. Open the event, click **Edit** and unfold **Show advanced options**.

![The two mail switches, under the advanced options](after.mail)

- **Send a reminder before the event**: a short reminder about three days beforehand.
- **Send a questionnaire afterwards**: five short questions the day after, about how it was.

With both off, the sign-up page does not ask for an address. Nothing would happen with it.

## What happens then

1. The address is stored encrypted. The database holds no readable address.
2. At the moment of sending, the app reads the address, sends the mail, and throws the address away.
3. Seven days after the event at the latest, every address is gone regardless, even if something went wrong.

You never see the address. It cannot be exported either. You only see how many mails were sent.

## You now see

On the event's page is the **Feedback** block with the answers to the questionnaire, and under it, per mail, a **Delivery** block with how many were sent and how many are still waiting.

![The Feedback block on the event's page](details.event.5)

**A sign-up says "no email"?** That person left no address. That is allowed, and nothing is sent to them.

**You want to send the reminder earlier?** In the Delivery block, click **Send reminders now**. Everyone still waiting gets it at once.

## Questions

**Are the answers tied to the sign-up?** No. The questionnaire is stored without a reference to who filled it in. We cannot see who answered what either.

**And with a roster?** There a volunteer may leave an address for a nudge before each shift. It stays as long as they take part, and goes as soon as they leave or switch the reminders off.

**What happens on archiving?** Mail that was still waiting is not sent, and the addresses for it are wiped at once.

**Where is the full text?** In the [privacy policy](/privacy). The code that does this is public.
