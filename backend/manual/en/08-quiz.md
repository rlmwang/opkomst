---
title: How do I make a quiz?
description: A quiz with right answers and a score, for an evening or a campaign.
audience: all
---
A quiz is a questionnaire with right answers. Whoever fills it in gets a score.

## Steps

1. Click the **Quiz** tile and then **New quiz**.

2. Type a title.

    ![The form with the title at the top](formulier.quiz.1)

3. Under **Questions**, click **Add question**. Type the question, put the answers under it and tick the right one. For a number you type the right number, with a margin if you like.

    ![The Questions block with a quiz question in it](formulier.quiz.2)

4. Give each question points. A hard question may be worth more.

5. Click **Create quiz** at the bottom.

## You now see

The quiz's page, with the link at the top right. Whoever opens it answers the questions and then sees their score. Everything is marked automatically, so open questions are out.

![The quiz's page with the link at the top right](details.quiz.2)

Under **Submissions** are the scores, with the name somebody gave.

**A question is refused?** Multiple choice has exactly one right answer. The message says which question it is.

## Questions

**Do players see which questions they got wrong?** Yes, unless you switch off **Show answers** under the advanced options. Do that when you play the same quiz twice in one evening.

**Can somebody change their answers afterwards?** No. A quiz's personal link only shows the score. Seeing the score and then changing the answers would be cheating.

**Does somebody have to give a name?** No. Without a name the score is listed as anonymous. Switch on **Name required** if you want to know who scored what.

**Can I use the same quiz twice?** Yes. The link keeps working as long as the quiz is not in the archive. Or make a copy by creating a new quiz.
