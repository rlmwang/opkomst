A date poll is a list of dates people choose from. You see at a glance which date the most people can make.

## Steps

1. Click the **Date poll** tile and then **New date poll**.

2. Type a title. A description is optional.

    ![The form with the title at the top](formulier.datepoll.1)

3. In the calendar under **Possible dates**, click the days you want to propose. To propose times on a day as well, click **+ time slot** on that day. Without a time slot the whole day counts.

    ![The calendar with the chosen dates](formulier.datepoll.2)

4. Click **Create date poll** at the bottom.

## You now see

The date poll's page, with the link at the top right. Share it with the group. Whoever opens it taps the moments they can make: **Yes** or **Maybe**. A moment somebody does not tap counts as "can't".

![The date poll's page with the link at the top right](details.datepoll.2)

**Somebody cannot see the dates?** On a phone the days sit side by side. A hint says there are more days to the right.

## Questions

**How do I see which date wins?** Under **Responses** on the page is a table: per date, how many people can, maybe can, or cannot. The best date is marked, with a 1st, 2nd and 3rd place.

**Can somebody leave a note?** Yes, there is a **Note** box. The notes are on your page under the table.

**Can somebody still change their answer?** Yes, with the personal link everyone sees after filling in. If you do not want that, switch off **Answers can be changed later** under the advanced options.

**Does a date poll ask for an email address?** No. A date poll sends no mail, so there is nothing to ask an address for.
