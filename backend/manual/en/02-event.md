An event is a gathering people sign up for. You make one in a minute, and it has its own page at once.

## Steps

1. Click the **Event** tile.

    ![The Event tile on the front page](welkom.1)

2. Click **New event**.

    ![The New event button above the list](welkom.2)

3. Type a title and pick a date. You may add a description and a location, but you do not have to.

    ![The form with a title and a date](welkom.3)

4. Click **Create event** at the bottom.

At step 3, pick a chapter too. The event belongs to that chapter, and only that chapter's organisers see it.
{: .organisation }

## You now see

Your event's page, with the link and the QR code at the top right. You share those with whoever should come. The next chapter says how.

![The new event's page, with the link at the top right](welkom.4)

**A message says the name is missing?** Type a title and click again.

**The form is gone without saving?** Go back to New event. What you typed is still there, because the app keeps a draft in your browser.

## Questions

**Can I change it later?** Yes. Open the event and click **Edit**. Anyone who already signed up stays signed up.

**What is under Show advanced options?** The switches: whether the event repeats, whether people can say what they will help with, and how they found you. Each switch has a line of explanation under it.

**Can I add a picture?** Yes, after saving. The edit page has an **Upload image** button. The picture appears on the sign-up page.

**What language is the sign-up page in?** You pick that at the bottom of the form under **Language**. Your own screen is separate from it.
