---
title: How do I make a questionnaire?
description: Ask your members or visitors questions, without anyone needing an account.
audience: all
---
A questionnaire is a list of questions with a link. Whoever opens the link fills it in, without an account.

## Steps

1. Click the **Questionnaire** tile and then **New questionnaire**.

2. Type a title.

    ![The form with the title at the top](formulier.form.1)

3. Under **Questions**, click **Add question**. Type the question and pick the kind: a star rating, a text box, multiple choice, multiple answers or a number. For multiple choice you type the answers under it.

    ![The Questions block with a question in it](formulier.form.2)

4. Click **Create questionnaire** at the bottom.

## You now see

The questionnaire's page, with the link at the top right. Whoever opens it sees the questions in the order you chose.

![The questionnaire's page with the link at the top right](details.form.2)

Under **Submissions** the answers land. Per question you see a summary: an average for a rating, a count for multiple choice, the text for a text question.

**A question is refused on saving?** A multiple choice question needs at least two answers, and every question needs a text. The message says which question it is.

## Questions

**Can I change the order?** Yes, with the arrows next to each question. Answers already in stay with their question.

**Can somebody change their answer later?** Yes, with the personal link everyone sees after sending. Switch off **Answers can be changed later** if you do not want that.

**Does somebody have to give a name?** No, unless you switch on **Name required**. A made-up name is always fine.

**Can I download the answers?** Yes, as CSV, with **Export CSV** above the submissions.
