A chore roster shares recurring chores among the people who sign up. The app builds the roster and keeps it fair.

## Steps

1. Click the **Roster** tile and then **New roster**.

2. Type a title, for instance the name of the evening or the room.

    ![The form with the title at the top](formulier.chore.1)

3. Under **Repeat**, choose how many weeks the cycle lasts. At 1 week every week is the same. At 2 weeks, week one has different chores from week two.

4. Under **Chores**, click **Add chore**. Give the chore a name, pick the days it falls on, and under **People per chore** how many people it takes.

    ![The Chores block with a few chores](formulier.chore.2)

5. Click **Create** at the bottom.

## You now see

The roster's page, with the link at the top right. Whoever opens it picks the chores they are willing to do and signs up. The app assigns them and keeps track of who has done the most.

![The roster's page with the link at the top right](details.chore.2)

Under **Roster** is the calendar. **Locked** means a shift is fixed. **Tentative** is an expectation that may still move.

**The roster stays empty?** Nothing appears until the first volunteer has signed up.

## Questions

**What can a volunteer do themselves?** Everything from their own page, which they get after signing up: pass on a shift, take an open one, cover for somebody, and leave. You need do nothing.

**How fair is it?** Every volunteer has a counter. The lowest is up next, and covering for somebody puts you ahead. Somebody who ticked two of six chores gets fewer turns than somebody who ticked all six.

**What is Days fixed in advance?** Under the advanced options is how many days ahead the roster is fixed. Beyond that it is an expectation. That is what lets somebody who joins later fold in quickly.

**Does the roster ask for an email address?** It may, but it does not have to. The address is used once to send the personal link and not kept.

With **Email reminders** switched on for the roster, the app keeps the address encrypted as long as somebody takes part, and drops it as soon as they leave. The chapter on email addresses says exactly how.
{: .organisation }

**Somebody new signs up. When do they join in?** From the first shift outside the fixed days. With **Fold in now** on the roster's page you slot them in earlier.
