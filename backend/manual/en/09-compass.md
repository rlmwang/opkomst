---
title: How do I make a compass?
description: Statements on axes, and everyone sees where they stand compared to each other.
audience: all
---
A compass is a questionnaire with statements on axes. Whoever fills it in sees where they stand, and you see where the group stands.

## Steps

1. Click the **Compass** tile and then **New compass**.

2. Type a title.

    ![The form with the title at the top](formulier.compass.1)

3. Under **Axes**, name the two axes and their two sides. For instance an Economy axis with left and right, and a Culture axis with conservative and progressive.

4. Under **Questions**, click **Add question**. For each answer you pick which side of which axis it pushes somebody towards.

    ![The Questions block with a statement in it](formulier.compass.2)

5. Click **Create compass** at the bottom.

## You now see

The compass's page, with the link at the top right. Whoever opens it answers the statements and then sees a map with a dot: that is where they stand.

![The compass's page with the link at the top right](details.compass.2)

On your page, under **Map**, is the same map with everyone on it.

**An axis is refused?** Every axis needs at least one question. Otherwise everyone lands on zero on that axis, and the message says so.

## Questions

**Does everyone see each other's name?** Yes, the name goes on the map and everyone who fills in the compass sees it. The page says so beforehand. Anyone who prefers to stay anonymous leaves the name empty.

**Can somebody change their answer later?** Yes, with the personal link. Switch off **Answers can be changed later** if you do not want that.

**How does the arithmetic work?** Each answer counts as a step towards a side of an axis. The dot is the mean of those steps per axis, so an axis with many questions weighs no more than one with few.

**Can I download the results?** Yes, as CSV, with **Export CSV** above the submissions.
