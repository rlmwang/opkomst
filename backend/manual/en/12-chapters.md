title: How do chapters and the agenda work?
description: Every chapter has its own agenda at its own address, and the settings say how far it reaches.

---
An organisation is made of chapters. Every chapter has a public agenda at its own address.

## Steps

1. Open the menu at the top right, click **Admin** and then **Chapters** at the top.

2. Type the name of a new chapter in the box at the top and press Enter.

    ![The box for adding a chapter](beheer.3)

3. Click the pencil on a chapter to change its name, its address and its home city. The address is the last part of the link to the agenda.

    ![A chapter in the list with the pencil next to it](beheer.4)

4. Click **Settings** at the top. Under **Agenda** you choose how many days ahead and back the public agenda shows.

    ![The agenda's window under Settings](beheer.5)

## You now see

A chapter's agenda is at the address that belongs to the chapter, as the pencil's page shows. Anyone can open that page. It lists what is coming up, and under it what just happened.

![A chapter's public agenda](after.afdelingen)

**An event is not on the agenda?** Open the event, click Edit and switch on **Show on the chapter agenda**. The sign-up link always works, even when that is off.

## Questions

**Who sees what?** An organiser sees only their own chapters' events. An admin sees everything. Which chapters somebody belongs to is set by an admin under Users.

**Can I delete a chapter?** Yes, with the bin. The chapter is hidden, and you can move its users and events to another chapter. Restoring is possible later too.

**Why is last month's event still on the agenda?** Because the window under Settings looks that far back. Lower **Show past** if you only want the recent past.

**What does the home city do?** When picking a location, addresses in that city come first.
