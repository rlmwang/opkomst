What is over goes into the archive. It leaves your list, but it is not gone.

## Steps

1. Go to the list, for instance **Events**. Every event has an **Archive** button.

    ![The Archive button on an event in the list](lijst.event.4)

2. Click it and confirm. The event leaves the list, and its public link stops working.

3. Want it back? Click **Archive** at the top.

    ![The Archive tab at the top of the list](lijst.event.5)

4. Click **Restore** on the event. It is back in the list with everything that belonged to it, and the link works again.

## You now see

The archive: everything you tidied away, each with the buttons **Restore** and **Delete permanently**.

![The archive with an archived event in it](after.archief)

**It is not in the archive?** Check that you are looking in the right list. Each kind of thing has its own archive: events, date polls, rosters and questionnaires separately.

## Questions

**How is this different from deleting?** Archiving can be undone and keeps everything. **Delete permanently** removes the event with all its sign-ups and answers, and that cannot be undone. That button is only in the archive.

**What happens to email addresses on archiving?** Mail that was still waiting is not sent, and the addresses for it go at once.

**Do I have to tidy up?** No. A long list is only inconvenient. A personal account does count only what is active, so tidying up makes room.

**Can I still look at an archived event?** Restore it first. In the archive you only see the name and the date.
