title: When something goes wrong

This chapter is for when something does not go as you thought. For each case it says what you see, what happened and what to do.

## The sign-in mail does not come

**You see:** the page says the link is on its way, but nothing arrives.

**What happened:** the mail is in your spam folder, or you made a typo in your address.

**What to do:** check your spam folder. Otherwise ask for a new link on the front page, and mind the address.

## The link says it has expired

**You see:** a page saying "Link expired".

**What happened:** the link was older than half an hour, or already used once. Every link works only once.

**What to do:** click **Request a new link** and use the newest mail.

## The date is wrong, and people have already signed up

**You see:** a wrong day or time on the event's page.

**What happened:** a typo when creating it.

**What to do:** click **Edit** and fix the date. The sign-ups stay. The app does not send a message about it, so say so in the group chat yourself.

## Something was archived by accident

**You see:** it is no longer in the list, and the link does not work.

**What happened:** somebody clicked Archive.

**What to do:** go to the **Archive** tab and click **Restore**. Everything is still there, and the link works again.

## Somebody cannot open the sign-up page

**You see:** the page says the link is no longer available.

**What happened:** the event is in the archive, or its date has passed.

**What to do:** restore it from the archive, or make a new event.

## Somebody lost their personal link

**You see:** somebody asks you to change or withdraw their sign-up.

**What happened:** the personal link is shown once, and not stored.

**What to do:** in the Sign-ups block, click **View sign-ups** and the link button next to the name. Send the new link to that person. Their page will say that an organiser did this.

## The form refuses to save

**You see:** a message at the top, for instance that the name is missing or that a question needs two answers.

**What happened:** the form is missing something it needs.

**What to do:** do what the message says and click again. What you typed stays.

## You see an empty list where something should be

**You see:** "No events yet", while you are sure you had one.

**What happened:** you are looking in the wrong list, or it is in the archive.

**What to do:** check in the menu at the top right that you are in the right section, and look in the archive.

As an organiser in a chapter you only see what belongs to your chapters. Ask an admin to put you in the right chapter if something is missing.
