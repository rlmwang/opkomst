There is no password to remember. You get a link in your mail every time, and the link signs you in.

## Steps

1. Go to the front page and type your email address at the bottom.

    ![The sign-in form at the bottom of the front page](inloggen.1)

2. Click **Send link**.

    ![The address is filled in, with Send link next to it](inloggen.2)

3. Open your mail and click the link. It works for half an hour, and only once.

    ![The page says the link is on its way](inloggen.3)

As an organiser in a chapter, the app asks your name the first time. Then you wait until an admin lets you in. Until then you see a page that says so.
{: .organisation }

## You now see

The page with the tiles: Event, Date poll, Roster, Questionnaire, Quiz and Compass. The menu is at the top right.

![The page with the tiles after signing in](welkom.1)

**No mail?** Check your spam folder. If it is not there either, just ask for a new link. The old one expires by itself.

**The link says it has expired?** It was older than half an hour, or already used once. Ask for a new one on the front page.

## Questions

**Why no password?** A password gets lost, reused, or leaked. A link in your mail works with what you already have, and there is nothing to leak.

**Can I stay signed in?** Yes. As long as you do not clear the browser, you stay signed in on that device. On another device you ask for a new link.

**I use a shared computer.** Sign out through the menu at the top right, at the bottom. That also clears anything you had not saved yet.
