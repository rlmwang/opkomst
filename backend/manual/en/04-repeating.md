---
title: How do I make an event come back?
description: A weekly evening or an eight-week course, an event that repeats itself.
audience: all
---
An event can come back, every week or every few weeks. You set it once and the app makes the next dates.

## Steps

1. Open the event's form and click **Show advanced options** at the bottom.

    ![The Show advanced options line under the form](formulier.event.3)

2. Switch on **Repeat**. The date and time you filled in at the top are the start.

    ![The Repeat switch, at the top of the unfolded options](formulier.event.4)

3. Choose how many weeks the pattern lasts. **Repeats every 1 weeks** brings it back every week. At 2 weeks, every other week.

4. In the grid under it, pick the days the event is on. One day a week is the usual case, but two is fine.

5. Choose whether it runs on or ends. **Ongoing, no end date** keeps making new dates. **For 8 weeks** stops after eight weeks.

6. Save.

## You now see

On the event's page the coming dates are listed, each with its own sign-ups. There is one link for the whole event. Whoever opens it picks the dates they will come.

**The dates are wrong?** Look at the date at the top of the form. That is the first time, and its weekday counts in the pattern.

## Questions

**Can I skip one date?** Change the pattern, or set an end and make a new event after it. Removing one date is not possible, because people may already have signed up for it.

**What happens to sign-ups when I change the pattern?** Dates that already have sign-ups stay. Dates without sign-ups that no longer fit the pattern go.

**How far ahead does the app make dates?** About three months for an ongoing event. Every night some are added, so the list never runs out.

**Can a roster do the same?** Yes, the roster uses the same pattern of weeks and days. See the chapter on sharing out the chores.
