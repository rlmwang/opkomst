Whoever signs up as an organiser waits first. An admin lets them in and picks their chapters.

## Steps

1. Open the menu at the top right and click **Admin**. The button carries a red number when somebody is waiting.

2. Whoever is waiting is in the list with an **Approve** button. Click it.

    ![The Approve button on somebody waiting](beheer.1)

3. Check the name and pick which chapters this person belongs to. Then click Approve.

4. At the top you switch between **Users**, **Chapters** and **Settings**.

    ![The tabs at the top of the admin pages](beheer.2)

## You now see

The list of all organisers, with the chapters next to each name. An admin has the label **admin**.

![The list of users](after.gebruikers)

**Somebody says they still cannot get in?** Check whether their name still has an Approve button. As long as it does, they are waiting.

## Questions

**How does somebody sign up?** On your organisation's front page, with an email address, like signing in. The first time the app asks for a name, and then the person waits for you.

**How do I make somebody an admin?** Click the pencil next to the name and switch on **admin**. An admin may do everything: let users in, make chapters and change settings.

**How do I remove somebody?** With the bin next to the name. That person cannot sign in from then on. If they sign up again later, they wait for approval again.

**Am I told when somebody is waiting?** Yes. Every Monday morning admins get a mail with who is waiting, when somebody is.
